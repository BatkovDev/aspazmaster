# AspazMaster

A digital marketplace connecting independent home-based food makers with customers in Kazakhstan.

AspazMaster is a platform designed to help independent food makers showcase their products, manage orders, and build their online presence without needing to develop their own websites.

The project focuses on making homemade food more accessible while providing small food businesses with simple digital tools to grow.

🌐 Website: "aspazmaster.com" (https://aspazmaster.com)
📍 Market: Kazakhstan
🚀 First launched: August 2026
🛠️ Status: MVP / Experimental Project

---

## Overview

Many independent food makers rely on Instagram, WhatsApp, and personal recommendations to reach customers and manage orders.

While these channels are useful, they often lack structured product catalogs, centralized order management, and dedicated storefronts.

AspazMaster aims to solve this problem by bringing discovery, storefronts, and order management into one platform.

The initial focus is on food makers and customers in Astana and Almaty, Kazakhstan.

## Key Features

### For Customers

- Browse homemade food products and independent makers.
- Discover products through categories, search, and filters.
- View detailed product listings and maker profiles.
- Add products to a shopping cart.
- Place orders directly through the platform.
- Track order progress through different fulfillment stages.

### For Food Makers

- Create and manage a digital storefront.
- Publish products with images, descriptions, and pricing.
- Organize products into categories.
- Receive and manage customer orders.
- Update order statuses.
- Share a personal storefront link with customers.
- Establish an online presence without building a separate website.

### Platform Capabilities

- Role-based user accounts.
- Google OAuth and email authentication.
- Product catalog and discovery.
- Shopping cart and order workflow.
- Order lifecycle management.
- Search engine optimization and social sharing metadata.
- Responsive web interface.

## How It Works

### 1. Discover

Customers explore products and independent food makers through the AspazMaster catalog.

### 2. Choose

Customers view product details, compare available options, and add items to their cart.

### 3. Order

An order is created and delivered to the corresponding maker for processing.

### 4. Fulfill

The maker manages the order through its lifecycle:

"Pending → Accepted → Ready → In Delivery → Completed"

### 5. Connect

Customers can return to their preferred makers through dedicated storefront pages.

## Business Model

AspazMaster was designed around an accessible, transaction-based model:

- Free onboarding for food makers.
- No upfront subscription required.
- Commission-based monetization for orders generated through the marketplace.
- 0% platform commission for orders originating from a maker's own shared storefront link.

This approach is intended to encourage adoption while allowing makers to maintain direct relationships with their customers.

## Vision

Our vision is to make digital commerce accessible to independent food entrepreneurs.

AspazMaster explores how a unified marketplace can help small businesses move beyond fragmented social media ordering and operate through a more structured digital environment.

The long-term concept includes improved product discovery, business analytics, promotional tools, and integrations that help makers reach relevant customers.

## Project Background

AspazMaster began as an attempt to address a practical problem in Kazakhstan's home-based food market: independent makers often have products and customers but lack convenient tools for managing their digital sales.

The first MVP was launched in August 2026, initially targeting Astana and Almaty.

The project also served as a foundation for exploring customer acquisition, marketplace dynamics, and AI-assisted demand discovery.
